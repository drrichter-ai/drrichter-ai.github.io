Privacy policy

Home AI OS is a private application used only by the household that runs it. It has no other users and is not offered to the public.

Google user data

  • The app requests only the calendar.app.created permission. It can create its own "Home AI OS" calendar and manage the entries on that calendar. It cannot read, change or delete any other calendar.
  • Entries are added only after a household member approves each one.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Data is processed and stored on the household's own server at home.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and the "Home AI OS" calendar can be deleted from Google Calendar.
